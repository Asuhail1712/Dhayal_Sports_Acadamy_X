# Client-Supplied Academy Photography

The original 45 photographs from the five supplied `Home` folders remain unchanged, including their WebP exports. Eleven replacements from `New Update` are exported separately with `-update` filenames and smaller responsive variants in `public/images/academy/`. There are now 35 active photographs across the five content groups; unused original training alternatives remain archived rather than being deleted.

`academy-photos.json` records the original import. `academy-photo-updates.json` records the replacement filenames, SHA-256 hashes, dimensions and output sizes. Replacements use only the supplied files; no additional stock photographs or generated edits are introduced.

The About collection still auto-advances every three seconds while at least 35% visible. Playback pauses off-screen, in hidden tabs, on pointer hover, during keyboard focus and when explicitly paused. Reduced-motion preferences disable automatic playback by default; users can opt in with Play. Labelled previous/next and pause/play buttons remain available. A fresh three-second countdown starts after visibility resumes or a manual change. Unique Training renders `PhotoFrame` directly, with exactly one photograph and no carousel controls, listeners or playback timer per card. All photo assets have descriptive alternative text and lazy loading, except detail-page images, which retain high loading priority.

Carousel controls, support-photo labels and the hero photo note share the navbar's glass tokens: 84% white, 26px blur, 1.4 saturation, a white border and inset highlight. Dark text and blue control hover states keep them readable over photography. Both standard and WebKit blur declarations are exported; browsers without blur support receive an opaque light fallback. Their dimensions, positions and controls are unchanged.

All photographs fill their containers edge to edge with `object-fit: cover`. Training frames now follow each replacement's natural aspect ratio, displaying the complete landscape without additional cropping or letterboxing. About, support, program and gallery frames retain their existing dimensions and focal crops. Newspaper coverage is top-aligned to retain the headline. There is no duplicate blurred background and photographs are never stretched.

Shot-specific focal positions prioritise faces, overhead strokes, feeding equipment and group placement. The same focus is used in gallery previews and matching program detail images. Support portraits retain role-specific framing. Existing container dimensions, responsive layouts and hover animations are unchanged; crops can vary with viewport size and gallery filtering. The original full photographs remain intact in the local assets.

The Pro Team photograph uses `50% 18%` focus on both cards and detail pages, lifting the player within the frame without cropping off the head. Whole-Athlete Support uses five complete image-and-content cards inspired by the services stack at `https://careplususa.framer.ai/services`. Each card sticks below the fixed navbar as the next card naturally slides upward over it. Native CSS sticky positioning controls the continuous movement: there are no scroll listeners, active-step thresholds, content crossfades, spacer timelines, nested scrollbars or wheel interception. Jump buttons use the cards' real flow heights and gaps, so both forward and backward navigation stay accurate after resizing. Phones stack each image above its content. Reduced-motion settings and very short viewports use a normal, unpinned list. The Champion message remains below the stack. Portraits are not assigned to named coaches based on appearance.

The stacked cards have translucent blue/green glass surfaces with 32px backdrop blur, luminous borders and inset highlights. They reuse the ProShop's soft background gradient and curved bottom swoosh, tinted to match each card; no 3D illustrations or additional image assets are added. Each card's icon tile, discipline label, heading, description and scroll prompt use its own blue or accessible dark-green accent rather than inheriting green from the section. Fine mouse pointers control spring-smoothed tilt and a subtle background-only light reflection, matching the other cards. An isolated surface keeps both the decoration and the light below the image and text, never as a foreground overlay. The effect is applied only to an inner surface; the untransformed sticky wrapper preserves scroll geometry and jump positions. Touch/coarse pointers and reduced-motion preferences disable the effect. The right-hand content has no separate background and sits directly on the shared glass surface. Unsupported blur engines receive a solid surface fallback. Standard and WebKit backdrop-filter declarations are retained in production CSS.

The hero, main coach cards, blogs, camps, partner sections, shop and five program tracks without supplied matches retain their existing imagery and content. The abstract decorative artwork in unrelated cards is unchanged.

`scripts/import-academy-photo-updates.cjs` recreates the replacement exports from a supplied source-directory argument, using Sharp through the existing `WORKSPACE_NODE_MODULES` convention. Only file-format optimization, orientation metadata correction and downsampling are applied during import; the supplied photographic content is unchanged.
